Trial Review Desk walkthrough transcript

All patient records, protocols, and AI suggestions in this portfolio demo are fictional.

From a possible match to a clear next step

Trial Review Desk helps clinical research coordinators review possible cancer trial matches. It brings the evidence, follow-up, and the reason for each decision into one workspace.

Review queue

Each row pairs a fictional patient with a trial protocol. The queue shows upcoming visits, review progress, and who owns each case.

Near-miss watchlist

Case one is waiting on a single test result. The watchlist shows the owner and the event that brings the case back. When a fictional report arrives, the coordinator rechecks the evidence. The follow-up stays open until it is resolved.

Exclusion and disposition

Case five shows a confirmed exclusion. The handoff stays blocked even when the inclusion checks pass. A structured reason explains why the match was closed.

Check the AI citation

In case seven, the simulated AI suggestion relies on an older note. The evidence panel shows both dated excerpts. The current note supports a different answer for this fictional criterion.

Record the correction

The coordinator selects Override, cites the current note, and tags the error as an outdated note. After saving, the case shows who changed the suggestion, when, and why.

Measure human review

The AI Evaluation Inspector keeps the original prediction and the correction visible. It counts suggestions reviewed, unique suggestions overridden, and total override actions. The benchmark uses a fixed set of fictional examples.

Preview the visit brief

The same Inspector entry opens a short visit brief. It shows reviewed evidence, the open question, and the next step. Selecting the citation opens the original excerpt.

See what happened after the match

Analytics shows how reviews progress and which protocol checks block cases. The goal is a clear next step for every possible match, with evidence and a reason the team can trace.
